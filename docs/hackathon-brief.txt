Common Technology Requirements
All hackathon projects should demonstrate the following capabilities:

1. Real-Time Conversational AI
Use Agora Conversational AI to power meaningful, low-latency, natural voice interactions. Agora RTC or Chat may also be incorporated wherever relevant.

2. Natural Voice Experiences
Integrate Murf AI Falcon TTS for natural and expressive voice output.

3. Agentic Workflows
Explore tool calling, APIs, and external integrations that enable your AI agent to perform actions beyond simply answering questions.

4. End-to-End User Journeys
Consider how your solution supports users before, during, and after the primary interaction, including relevant follow-up actions.

5. Working Demonstration
Showcase a functional prototype that demonstrates the interaction, action execution, and resulting outcome.

Think Beyond the Conversation
The tracks and example solutions are intended to inspire innovation, not limit it.

We encourage participants to think beyond traditional voice assistants and explore how real-time conversational AI can become an intelligent, action-oriented experience.

Consider how your solution can:

Move from Conversation to Action: Use tools, APIs, and connected services to turn conversations into meaningful actions.
Deliver Complete User Journeys: Include preparation, real-time engagement, task execution, and follow-up.
Demonstrate Contextual Intelligence: Remember relevant information and adapt to individual user needs.
Support Multi-Step Decision-Making: Handle complex workflows, error scenarios, and human intervention wherever appropriate.
Introduce Multimodal Experiences: Experiment with voice, video, visual interfaces, and physical devices.
Solve Real-World Problems: Build practical, scalable, and thoughtfully designed solutions.
Participants are welcome to go beyond the suggested use cases, experiment with additional technologies, and introduce creative capabilities that demonstrate the broader potential of Agentic AI.

The goal is not simply to build an AI that talks, but to build an AI that gets things done.
